**Sensing and contact regressor.** At each 8 ms tick, `ForceSensing` subtracts estimated filtered probe inertia from measured force. It simulates `a=(u−y−(t1+t2)v)/(t1 t2)` at 0.5 ms, filters `m_hat a` at 1 ms, and interpolates acceleration history for the estimated sensor delay. For the force filter, `a=1−afc`, `afc=1−exp(−2π·30·0.001)`, `ns=8`. Each valid interval yields

`z_j=F[j+1]−a^8 F[j]`,  
`Y_j=Σ_(m=1..8) afc a^(8−m) (y_H(j,m)−y_H,end)`.

The intra-tick `y_H(j,m)` interpolates measured encoder endpoints with the estimated servo exponential; intervals with either endpoint force ≤0.5 N are excluded. **`Y` is real**, constructed after the H transformation, not a complex regression variable. H applies the normalized phase-leading difference `y_H[j]=y0+((y[j]−y0)+tau_h Δy/0.008)/mag_h`, where `diff=(1−exp(−iωTc))/Tc`, `tau_h=tan(phi)/max(Im(diff)−tan(phi)Re(diff),1)`, and `mag_h=|1+tau_h diff|`. The phase is obtained online from `arg((carrierᵀ Mz)/(carrierᵀ MY))`, with `M=I−(I+rho D4ᵀD4)^−1`, clipped to `[0,0.5]` rad and exponentially averaged with a 2 s time constant. It updates after a sufficiently precise estimate and a full, uninterrupted window. **Window estimator.** For up to 24 uninterrupted intervals, at least eight, the implemented target is

`min_(k,d) ||z−kY−d||² + 2 rho s² Σ h_1.345((D4 d)_i/s)`,  
`s=0.04/sqrt(rho)`, `rho=[2 sin(π·14·0.008)]^(−8)=19.6248`,

where `h_c(u)=u²/2` for `|u|≤c`, otherwise `c|u|−c²/2`. Six cold-start IRLS solves use `S=(I+rho D4ᵀ W D4)^−1`, `M=I−S`, `k=(YᵀMz)/(YᵀMY)`, `d=S(z−kY)`, and `W_ii=min(1,1.345/|(D4d)_i/s|)` between solves. Banded linear solves process `Y,z` together. The returned values are clipped `k_hat` in `[200,300000]` N/m, `sigma=0.796·0.04/sqrt(YᵀMY)`, and `chi=0.551·rᵀMr/[0.04² max(m−5,1)]`, using final frozen weights. `d` is an internal IRLS quantity, **not an estimator output fed to the motion tracker**; sigma and chi are frozen-weight surrogate statistics. **Motion feed-forward.** This predicts an accumulated equivalent displacement `Z`, not the window’s `d`. With `decay=a^8`, `F_inv=(F_j−decay F_(j−1))/(1−decay)`, and matched interval encoder displacement `y_m`, H converts increments using `k_c=k_good(max(F,0.3)/F_at_accept)^0.206`, falling back to `k_bar` if unavailable:

`δc=(mag_h ΔF_inv/k_c + (tau_h/Tc)δc_prev)/(1+tau_h/Tc)`,  
`Z←Z+δc−Δy_m`.

A two-model constant-acceleration IMM tracks `Z`: state `[s,v,a]`, smooth and maneuver white-jerk intensities `0.0003218513` and `3.335`, stay probability `0.9999`, and measurement variance `(0.04/k_ref)²` (`r_scale=1`, no variance floor or chi inflation). It extrapolates `L=(ndel+0.5)Tc` and returns `q=s(L)+tau_hat v(L)`. Normally `dq=k_good(q−q_prev)`, hence the final command subtracts the predicted displacement increment. Before `k_good`, the prediction is continually re-anchored, preventing startup feed-forward. If `k_check/k_good>1.5` or `<1/1.5`, `dq=0`; prediction history still advances.
