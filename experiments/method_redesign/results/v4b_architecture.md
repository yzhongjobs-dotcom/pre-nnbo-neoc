- **motion**?Same FINAL window/good-gain acceptance b<=ln(1.2), force-equivalent IMM measurement, encoder endpoints, V1 online servo ID. Prediction L=(ndel+0.5)*Tc=12ms initially plus tau_hat*predicted velocity. dq=k_good*Delta_eta_p; applied increment=-dq/k_good. No new FF scaling, clipping or tracker tuning.
- **residual**?Virtual coordinate y_r=y-a_ff; c_r=c-offset_ff. a_ff is causal first-order servo propagation of the actual sent FF offsets with V1 tau/delay, not force truth. F=k*y_r+(g+k*a_ff), so nuisance drift is post-FF residual. Physical log is used for V1 and FINAL observer; virtual log is used only for C1. Simply retaining physical y would leave full environmental drift in the C1 fit.
- **future_input**?Conservative allowed option: Current FF increment is supplied as a known input to physical command allocation, then subtracted before residual-servo propagation to keep the residual coordinate consistent; all SUBSEQUENT FF-command increments are zero, i.e. the newly sent offset is held. The final VMAX slew cap is applied to base+known FF; the result minus known FF drives the residual servo. FF must not be added again to force propagation, since residual drift already includes its response. Transport delay/servo are retained; equilibrium robustness probes are unchanged. C1 polynomial residual forecast absorbs the past FF actuator response; its future continuation is an approximation, not an exact independent predictor of anatomical motion. No 128ms IMM force table.
- **sent_anchor**?After physical clipping, FF offset is rebased to actual_command-base_command each tick, so unsent FF is not accumulated. This is state bookkeeping, not additional motion compensation during loss. Physical/current FINAL observer remains unchanged. The residual split uses allocated, actually sent offsets.
- **GN**?C1 cost, residual stacking and GN numerical solver unchanged; nominal rollout adds known current FF and final command slew cap. Original 16-state path is exactly the original kernel. Same four decisions logD/KPF/u0/u1, cost, priors, probes, bounds, warm start, H=16, iters=1 source default. ru=0 default; V4 historical tuned ru is not carried into original C1+V1.
- **noOpt**?Same FF and residual estimation, same C1 recovery and integral rule. Each normal tick uses D,KPF=rule(k_C1), u0=u1=0 plus C1 dither. Legacy iters=0 alone is NOT sufficient: it freezes previous gains rather than applying the prior rule. Recovery/exit tick retains its continuous command.
- **stiffness**?C1 uses its own residual-coordinate stiffness for prediction/gains/excitation. FINAL observer independently fits physical data and accepts k_good for force-to-motion conversion. No substitution between the two stiffnesses. Algebraically separated channels remain dynamically coupled through the physical force and position.
- **recovery**?FF increments disabled at F<0.5; existing offset held continuously. IMM clears below0.3. Observer fits in contact even during C1 settling; FF may resume at F>=0.5 with accepted gain and initialized IMM, independently of C1 mode. C1 force recovery target, damping and 12-tick exit unchanged. On C1 recontact exit, drift/window/warm-start reset via C1 and IMM/increment anchor cleared. Accepted stiffness retained, matching FINAL loss handling.
- **startup**?V4b and noOpt use source-recommended k0=30000 N/m instead of historical C1=5000. Triggered by training-only startup blocking. Original C1+V1 kept unchanged. Sensitivity audit distinguishes this implementation change from a clean one-factor FF ablation.
- **interface**?V4b direct executor mirrors C1 admittance and consumes fresh force exactly like plant law; GN uses completed rows as original C1. FINAL observer consumes provisional current row as Proposed does. V4b position speed cap is same as Proposed. Original C1 gain interface retains its historical force-sampling ordering. Therefore sensor availability matches, but the original execution timing and saturation conventions are not identical; sensitivity is reported, not hidden.
