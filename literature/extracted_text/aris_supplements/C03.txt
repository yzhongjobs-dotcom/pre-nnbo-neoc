                III. PARAMETER DETERMINATION
   This section elaborates on the determination of the PM’s struc-
tural parameters via FEA and experimental evaluation. In accor-
dance with the analytical relationships derived in Section II-B,
a parameterized CAD model of the PM was established, as
depicted in Fig. 1. For the convenience of rapid prototyping and
experimental testing, the PM was fabricated via 3-D printing
using acrylonitrile butadiene styrene (ABS) as the raw mate-
rial. Most structural parameters were determined according to
assembly constraints and the manufacturing capacity of the 3-D
printer.
   In this case study, R and r are set to 45.0 mm and 10.0 mm
(see Fig. 2), respectively, with the overall dimension of the PM
controlled within a reasonable range to avoid excessive sizing.
As specified in Section II-B, the maximum angular deformation
in the simulation θn,max is defined as γ, which is strictly larger
than the maximum angular deformation within the constant-
torque regime. The variable parameters optimized for constant-
torque performance are determined as αn and lp . Considering the
3-D printing manufacturing resolution, the minimum adjustment
increments for these structural parameters are set to 0.1◦ and
0.1 mm, respectively.
   Based on previous studies [37], [38], [39], [42], the constant-
torque regime cannot be accurately predicted via FEA simula-
tion alone, which is mainly attributed to prediction deviations
of the buckling mode. Given the merits of 3-D printing in facile
