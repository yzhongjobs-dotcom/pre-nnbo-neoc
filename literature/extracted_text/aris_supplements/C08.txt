                    Wenxin Zhu , Member, IEEE, Qinyuan Ren , Senior Member, IEEE,

   Abstract—In robotic physical interaction tasks, passive
control is significant to guarantee robust stability of the
systems with unknown environments. In this article, a
safety-critical passive control approach via control barrier
functions (CBFs) is proposed for robotic soft tissue cut-
ting tasks. To achieve stable cutting motion, this approach
builds upon unified force-impedance control enhanced with
the energy tanks that constrain the system energy. The
energy tanks are formulated with optimization theory to
constrain both time-varying energy and power flow, incor-
porated with high-order time-varying CBFs (HoTvCBFs). To
address unknown higher order derivatives in HoTvCBFs
under external disturbances, a time-varying projection-
to-state safe differentiator-adaptive HoTvCBF (tPSSf-DA–
HoTvCBF) approach is developed. The proposed approach
employs an input-to-state stable (ISS) differentiator to esti-
