                                        development of an optimization-based control framework for an
                                        ultrasound-guided assistive robot to perform scoliosis assessment.
                                        A conventional procedure for scoliosis assessment with ultrasound
                                        imaging typically requires a medical practitioner to slide an
                                        ultrasound probe along a patient’s back. To automate this type of
                                        procedure, we need to consider multiple objectives, such as con-
                                        tact force, position, orientation, energy, posture, etc. To address
                                        the aforementioned components, we propose to formulate the
                                        control framework design as a quadratic programming problem
                                        with each objective weighed by its task priority subject to a set
                                        of equality and inequality constraints. In addition, as the robot
                                        needs to establish constant contact with the patient during spine
                                        scanning, we incorporate variable impedance regulation of the
