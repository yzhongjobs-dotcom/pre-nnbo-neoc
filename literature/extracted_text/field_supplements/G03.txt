ARTICLE              INFO                             ABSTRACT

Keywords:                                             When robots interact with their environment, managing the physical interaction safely and accurately is
Admittance control                                    critical. Impedance and admittance control allow such interaction and have been fundamental to robotic
Model predictive control                              control for decades. For challenging applications, it is often necessary to implement a time-varying impedance
Physical human–robot interaction
                                                      profile, which necessitates more complex conditions to guarantee stability. This paper introduces a method
                                                      to implement variable admittance control with explicit passivity constraints using non-linear model predictive
                                                      control (NMPC). The dynamics of the impedance parameters are directly included in the predictive controller.
                                                      Contrary to previous approaches, the computed control signals enforce simultaneously the passivity condition
                                                      and the constraints, on both the robot state and the control signals.
