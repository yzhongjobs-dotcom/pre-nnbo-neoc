# Reviewed transferable mechanisms

Purpose: retain mechanisms and sources for later design so useful methodological leads are not lost when old routes are removed. None is predetermined as the final method. This record adds no novelty verdict or method ranking. See the [literature index](../literature/literature_index.md) and source JSON for reading scope.

| Mechanism and source | Potential use | Adaptation checks |
|---|---|---|
| NNBO critic/HJB and inverse position-reference mapping, [paper](../literature/papers/NNBO.pdf) | Organize contact optimal control into executable references | The paper uses preidentified HC and position references; it does not directly output four K/D gains |
| NEOC, [paper](../literature/papers/NEOC.pdf); RTI, [Diehl et al., CDC2005](https://skoge.folk.ntnu.no/prost/proceedings/cdc-ecc05/pdffiles/papers/1186.pdf) | Reuse solutions/derivatives after parameter changes; separate preparation and feedback | Smoothness, regularity, update size, complete cost and comparisons with simple Newton/chord methods; RTI reading covered only §II–III |
| YOPO, [local paper](../literature/papers/method_transfer/YOPO.pdf) | Move solution effort offline, generate candidates directly online, and guide learning with task cost | Original task is flight-trajectory planning with guidance learning; contact information, actual impedance actions and nondifferentiable simulation require adaptation |
| PPO and VICES, [VICES author manuscript](https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.08880) | Train impedance-action policies without contact derivatives | Sample count, observation sufficiency, task reward and transfer; impedance RL already exists, so combining algorithms is not itself a contribution |
| Model learning plus prediction/actor–critic, E37/E38/E40 and Matschek F04, [local material](../literature/literature_index.md) | Predict interaction consequences, approximate decisions through learning or update models online | Reference/damping/stiffness actions and model assumptions differ; borrow and improve capabilities accordingly |
| Internal-state contact models and joint estimation, Moreira F10, Tiezzi F05, [CARE](https://www.roboticsproceedings.org/rss10/p51.pdf) | Handle relaxation/motion and material–geometry confounding | Precedents already exist in this field; whether observers/MHE/spatial moments provide required task benefits remains to be checked |
| Task-cost training, adjoint gradients, control blocking and warm starts, [implementations](../experiments/experiment_index.md) | Reduce irrelevant label error, gradient overhead and online work | These are mature tools with reusable prototype implementations; no paper contribution has yet been established from them |
| Passivity/energy and stable variable impedance, Fu, E06/E16/E47/E49 and SNEUM F06 | Handle energy injection from time-varying gains and execution constraints | Positive K/D does not automatically ensure time-varying stability; an energy ledger does not guarantee hard force safety; certificates require their conditions |
| Dual control/future-observation planning, [JMLR2016](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v17/15-162.html), TASE2026 DCEE; value-map reuse, [cross-field lead](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-021-25123-3) | Account for actions changing later information/decisions, or reuse value structure across environment changes | The old two-stage prototype had small benefits and high cost and is no longer the default route; applicability of the cross-field map to contact is not derived. Only the JMLR abstract was checked, which cannot support citing its entire theory |

SRL-VIC (DOI10.1109/LRA.2024.3396368) and Zanella et al. (DOI10.1049/cth2.12558) also use safety/energy-assisted RL. Earlier reports recorded relevant original passages but did not audit every proof. Retain these as traceable neighboring work; removing the mistaken PPO experiment does not remove the literature basis for learning control.
