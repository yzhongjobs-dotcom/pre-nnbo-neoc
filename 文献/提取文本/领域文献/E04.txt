        B. Experiment Studies
           The experiments are implemented on a 6-Dof robot platform
        (Fig. 8(a)), which works in a position mode. The controller of
        robot is an Industrial Personal Computer (IPC) with operation
        system Ubuntu22.04, equipped with RT kernel Linux-5.10.180-
        rt88. The algorithm is implemented with C++ code, with 1 ms
        control period based on the EtherCAT bus. The 6D force sensor
        ATI Mini45 is equipped on the robot end-effector to measure
        contact force, which communicate with robot controller via
        TCP/IP, with communication cycle 1 ms. Also, four methods
        CIPC, TAIPC, TAIPC-PD and NAPC are performed in different
