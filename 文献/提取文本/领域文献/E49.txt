         mounted at the end-effector of a KUKA iiwa 14 robot,
         equipped with an ATI-Mini40 force-torque sensor (Fig. 4).                           In this paper we propose a design method for filters
                                                                                          that permit the tracking of impedance profiles for variable
                                                                                          impedance control, while guaranteeing passivity. Two filters
                                                                                          are derived based on two candidate storage functions. The
                                                                                          proposed passivity filters were validated in simulation and in
                                                                                          an experimental setup featuring a KUKA iiwa collaborative
                                                                                          robot executing a needle insertion task.
                                                                                             Future work aims at expanding the proposed filter design
                                                                                          with other passivity conditions in search of even less con-
                                                                                          servative passivity guarantees. Also, it can be investigated
