2. Venue calibration 核心论文
======================================================================

A. Wijayarathne et al.
Real-Time Deformable-Contact-Aware Model Predictive Control for Force-Modulated Manipulation
IEEE Transactions on Robotics, 2023
DOI: 10.1109/TRO.2023.3286070
角色：复杂但单主线的 soft-contact dynamics → constrained force/motion TO → real-time MPC。

B. Pollayil et al.
Choosing Stiffness and Damping for Optimal Impedance Planning
IEEE Transactions on Robotics, 2023
DOI: 10.1109/TRO.2022.3216078
角色：focused optimal impedance planning；证明顶刊不要求大型方法堆栈。

C. Fu et al.
Human-Inspired Active Compliant and Passive Shared Control Framework for Robotic Contact-Rich Tasks in Medical Applications
IEEE Transactions on Robotics, 2025
DOI: 10.1109/TRO.2025.3548493
角色：system-level interconnection、whole-system passivity 与 medical-contact evidence。

D. Haddadin & Shahriari
Unified Force-Impedance Control
The International Journal of Robotics Research, 2024
DOI: 10.1177/02783649241249194
角色：general unified framework；force regulation、impedance、contact transition 与 passivity。

E. Piedra, Jeffrey, Khatib
Explicit Force-Based Control Strategies for Compliant Robot-Assisted Ultrasound Imaging
The International Journal of Robotics Research, 2026
DOI: 10.1177/02783649261441635
角色：same-problem simpler architecture；直接力/力矩控制显著降低 phase lag 和动态 force error。

F. Jin et al.
Constrained Variable Impedance Control Using Quadratic Programming
ICRA, 2022
DOI: 10.1109/ICRA46639.2022.9812210
角色：focused online constrained K update。

G. Beber et al.
A Passive Variable Impedance Control Strategy With Viscoelastic Parameters Estimation of Soft Tissues for Safe Ultrasonography
ICRA, 2024
DOI: 10.1109/ICRA57147.2024.10610167
角色：最直接 target-domain prior；offline tissue characterization → online K QP → energy tank。
关键全文事实：参数表征为 offline，不是 online；论文把 online estimation 列为未来需求。

H. Reuss et al.
End-to-End Learning of Hybrid Inverse Dynamics Models for Precise and Compliant Impedance Control
RSS, 2022
DOI: 10.15607/RSS.2022.XVIII.066
角色：一个清晰机制直接产生 lower-gain precise/compliant robot capability 的 RSS morphology。

3. Active identification / information strongest priors
======================================================================

A. Liang, Saxena, Kroemer
Learning Active Task-Oriented Exploration Policies for Bridging the Sim-to-Real Gap
RSS, 2020
链接：https://www.roboticsproceedings.org/rss16/p085.pdf
已覆盖：以 downstream task regret 训练 active exploration，识别 task-relevant continuous parameters。
R15 必须区别：连续扫描同环执行、soft-contact belief、executable impedance 和安全约束。

B. Zhang, Zhou, Vasudevan
Provably-Safe, Online System Identification
RSS, 2025
链接：https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.21486
已覆盖：安全激励轨迹、机器人约束/避障、鲁棒参数界。
R15 必须区别：未知软接触、反馈阻抗决策、诊断扫描与 downstream decision sufficiency。

C. Sathyanarayan & Abraham
Behavior Synthesis via Contact-Aware Fisher Information Maximization
RSS, 2025
链接：https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.12214
已覆盖：contact-aware Fisher information 与 contact-seeking behavior synthesis。
R15 必须区别：只减少会改变 K decision 的不确定性，并闭合到 applied impedance/task outcome。

D. Liu et al.
Online Adaptive Identification and Switching of Soft Contact Model Based on ART-II Method
ICRA, 2022
DOI: 10.1109/ICRA46639.2022.9811740
已覆盖：soft contact model online identification/switching。
R15 不能把 online soft-contact identification 单独当创新。

5. 新对话最低必读原文
======================================================================

为继续推导而非重复综述，最低应回查：

1) T-RO 2023：contact dynamics、state/action、constraints、ADMM/MPC 与 low-level execution；
2) Beber ICRA 2024：offline characterization、HC fitting、QP、tank 和作者明确 limitation；
3) NNBO：EWRLS、augmented state、HJB action、critic、force-to-impedance realization；
4) Piedra IJRR 2026：explicit force/moment architecture 及其对动态/异质组织的性能；
5) RSS 2020：task regret 与 exploration→identification→task policy 结构；
6) RSS 2025 Safe SysID：安全激励和参数集合保证；
7) RSS 2025 Contact-aware FIM：contact information measure 与行为生成。

只有在上述证据不足以回答某个具体接口时，才继续检索；不得重新无边界扩展领域综述。

======================================================================
6. Comparator 使用顺序
======================================================================

Strongest novelty priors：Beber、T-RO 2023、NNBO、RSS task-oriented exploration。

Same-problem alternative：Piedra IJRR 2026 explicit force control。

Simpler method-fit competitors：fixed robust K、current-step K-QP、certainty-equivalent estimator + reactive K。

Same-venue morphology：Pollayil T-RO、Wijayarathne T-RO、Beber ICRA、Reuss RSS、Piedra IJRR。

Boundary priors：Haninger、Anand、Xue、Roveda。

最终 novelty、necessity、complexity 和 venue 判断必须按上述顺序完成，禁止只选有利对照。
