Article history:                                Robotic tasks which involve uncertainty – due to variation in goal, environment configuration, or

Accepted 13 April 2023                          to individual uncertainties have been proposed,  e.g., realizing intention detection or uncertainty-
Available online 23 April 2023
                                       aware learning from demonstration. However, isolated methods cannot address the wide range of
Keywords:                                      uncertainties jointly present in many tasks.
Impedance control                            To improve generality, this paper proposes a model predictive control (MPC) framework which
Model predictive control                        plans both trajectory and impedance online, can consider discrete and continuous uncertainties,
Human–robot interaction                        includes safety constraints, and can be efficiently applied to a new task. This framework can consider
Gaussian processes                             uncertainty from: contact constraint variation, uncertainty in human goals, or task disturbances. An
Intention detection                            uncertainty-aware task model is learned from a few (≤3) demonstrations using Gaussian Processes.
                                              This task model is used in a nonlinear MPC problem to optimize robot trajectory and impedance
                                            according to belief in discrete human goals, human kinematics, safety constraints, contact stability, and
                                         frequency-domain disturbance rejection. This MPC formulation is introduced, analyzed with respect to
                                              convexity, and validated in co-manipulation with multiple goals, a collaborative polishing task, and a
                                               collaborative assembly task.
